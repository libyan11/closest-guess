# Closest Guess

## ▶ [Play the game](https://libyan11.github.io/closest-guess/)

https://libyan11.github.io/closest-guess/

A number-guessing party game in your browser, on phone or computer. How tall is the Burj Khalifa? How many bones are in your body? Nobody knows exactly, so the closest guess wins. Play online with friends or against the computer. Works offline against the computer after the first visit. Part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

**Play with friends:** one person taps *Create a room* and shares the 4-letter code or link. 2 to 8 players; the host can add computer players and pick 5 to 20 questions and the time limit.

**Rules**
- Every question has a number for an answer. Type your guess before the clock runs out.
- Closest guess gets 3 points, next 2, next 1. Furthest away gets nothing. Ties share.
- Hit the answer exactly for 2 extra points.
- Shortcuts: 12k = 12,000, 1.5m = 1,500,000.
- Most points after all the questions wins.
